Om boligen
This house is perfectly suited to a 3 generation family.
It has been used as a student house, then a foreign worker's house with the same 5 people for the past 5 years.
Everything in the house works, but the whole property needs considerable upgrading to bring it up to modern standards. I have not spent time or money on maintenance in the past few years because the intention was to sell the entire property to a developer who might well clear away the house and the barn.
More precise information is in the Tilstandsrapport attached.
On a more positive note, the people who have lived here found it comfortable, convenient and peaceful.
Beliggenhet
There is a bus stop next to the property, a new Kiwi across the carpark, a sports hall, skiing and frisbee golf a short walk away. Hafjell is 10 mins drive, Lillehammer centre 5 mins.  The E 6 passes nearby. The kindergarten and school are over the road (which as an underpass next to the property).
Please note that the attached google map is out of date; the old school on the east side of the road is now a Kiwi supermarket and carpark.
Annet
This property now has 'sentral formal' building regulations. This opens up several possible types of development...blocks of flats; private 'tompter' for selling; a large self-contained student residence for the university which is a bike ride away; a småbruk with cafe and shop?.....see the last of the pictures which shows the kommune's proposal for development as flats.
As a medium term investment, new accommodation will become needed for the thousands of people who will come to staff the proposed hospital at Moelv, 30 mins away.
No acceptable offers have been made.
12th September...I have started the process of dividing up the property so that the house, barn and land can be purchased separately.
I expect the process to take some weeks, but for your information, the house will be on offer for 2.2mill, the barn 0.3mill (if bought with the house) and the land at 3.5mill.
Thus the entire property will have come down in price.
